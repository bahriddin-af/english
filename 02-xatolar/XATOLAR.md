# Xatolar daftari

Har kuni sessiyadan keyin shu yerga qo'shaman.
**Yakshanba kuni** sherigim bilan birga ko'rib chiqamiz.

## Qanday ishlaydi

- Xato tuzatilganda `Holat` ustunini `✅ yo'qoldi` ga o'zgartiraman
- 3 haftadan ortiq turgan xato — **qattiq xato**, alohida mashq kerak
- Har oyning oxirida yo'qolgan xatolarni pastga ko'chiraman

## A2 darajada eng ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

Bularni oldindan bilib turing — o'zingizni ushlashingiz osonroq bo'ladi:

| Xato | To'g'ri | Izoh |
|---|---|---|
| `He work in a bank` | `He works in a bank` | 3-shaxs `-s` |
| `I didn't went` | `I didn't go` | `didn't` + oddiy fe'l |
| `I working now` | `I am working now` | `am/is/are` tushib qolgan |
| `I am agree` | `I agree` | `agree` fe'l, sifat emas |
| `I have 25 years` | `I am 25` | yosh — `to be` bilan |
| `Yesterday I go` | `Yesterday I went` | o'tgan zamon shakli |
| `I very like it` | `I like it very much` | `very` fe'lni kuchaytirmaydi |
| `People is` | `People are` | `people` — ko'plik |
| `I go to home` | `I go home` | `home` oldiga `to` kelmaydi |
| `Explain me` | `Explain to me` | `explain` + `to` |
