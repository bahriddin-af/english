# IT ingliz tili

Ishda va intervyuda kerak bo'ladigan iboralar. **A2 darajaga moslangan** —
oddiy va qisqa. Har bir iborani **o'z ishim haqidagi gap** bilan mashq qilaman.

Yangi ibora qo'shilganda `Holat` ustuni ☐ bo'ladi, gapirishda erkin ishlatganimda ✅.

---

## 1. Intervyu — o'zim haqimda

| Ibora | Mening gapim | Holat |
|---|---|---|
| `I am a ... developer.` | `I am a .NET developer.` | ☐ |
| `I have ... years of experience.` | `I have [X] years of experience.` | ☐ |
| `I work at ...` | `I work at a software company.` | ☐ |
| `I usually work with ...` | `I usually work with C# and PostgreSQL.` | ☐ |
| `My main task is ...` | `My main task is to build APIs.` | ☐ |

## 2. Tushunmaganda

| Ibora | Holat |
|---|---|
| `Sorry, can you repeat that, please?` | ☐ |
| `Can you say it more slowly, please?` | ☐ |
| `What do you mean by ...?` | ☐ |
| `Let me think for a second.` | ☐ |
