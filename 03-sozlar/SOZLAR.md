# So'zlar

## Qoida

So'zni **yakka emas, gap ichida** olaman. Va gap **men haqimda** bo'lishi shart.

```
❌ decide — qaror qilmoq
✅ I decided to change my job last year.
```

**Manba:** monolog paytida topolmagan so'zlarim. Bular eng qimmatlisi —
mening hayotimdan kelib chiqqan, shuning uchun yodda qoladi.

**Kunlik chegara: 5–7 ta.** Ko'proq olsam — hech biri qolmaydi.

---

## Anki kartasi shakli

Old tomonda **o'zbekcha so'z emas**, bo'sh joyli inglizcha gap:

| Old tomon | Orqa tomon |
|---|---|
| `The meeting was really ________.` (charchatadigan) | `exhausting` |

Bu miyani eslatishga emas, **ishlatishga** majbur qiladi.

## Dasturchi uchun kerakli fe'llar

Ishim haqida gapirganda eng ko'p kerak bo'ladigan fe'llar —
bularni birinchi navbatda avtomatlashtiraman:

`build` · `fix` · `deploy` · `merge` · `review` · `test` · `debug` ·
`refactor` · `release` · `break` · `crash` · `handle` · `store` ·
`fetch` · `update` · `maintain` · `migrate` · `roll back` · `ship`

**Mashq:** har biriga o'z ishim haqida gap tuzaman —
`Yesterday I deployed the new version and it broke the payment page.`
